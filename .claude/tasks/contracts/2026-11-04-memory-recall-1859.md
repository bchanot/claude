## CLARIFICATIONS
Pass A (gaps found by the STEP 0d read-before, put to the user at the STEP 1 brainstorm; answers land `[gated]`):
- The classifier label is private to model-router (`st.turnMain`, never stored or published) and the classifier never runs on prompts its keyword rules already map (`plan|design|architecture…` → plan, `why|explain|review|audit…` → reflect), nor on slash commands: a trigger "classifier labels design/analysis" read literally misses exactly the design/analysis prompts that say so.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (seed, enriched at STEP 3 `[gated]`)
1. Intent recall: on the qualifying prompts (trigger decided at the brainstorm), the mod ranks live registry entries by BM25 over title + lesson + body, asks a small model to keep ≤ 3, bounded in time, and attaches them as context (ID + title + lesson line); any failure or timeout attaches nothing and passes the prompt through unchanged.
2. Tool-error match: a captured tool failure whose error line shares a 4-gram with a live BLK/LRN entry attaches that entry (ID + title + lesson line) as context; the common-phrase collision (ENOENT → LRN-056) no longer fires.
3. Superseded entries are never injected; the superseded reader is decided at the brainstorm and its verdicts agree with lib/memory.sh on this repo's registries (an independent oracle, LRN-237).
4. Budget: at most ~1.5k chars of injected recall per turn; an entry is injected at most once per context (reset on /clear and compaction).
5. Session digest: `hooks/session-start.sh` prints open BLK (status parser), actionable TODO and the last journal line on `source` startup|compact|clear, bounded in size; runs unchanged with `</dev/null` and in projects without registries or TODO.
6. `memory_pending` lists earlier-session milestones (marked `(earlier session)`) after the current session's.
7. Every hook fails open; the `~/.claude/memory.json` off switch covers the read side; `claude plugin validate --strict`, `claude plugin test` (mods suite) and `make test` are green.
8. Proof: recall precision measured on ≥ 20 real prompts and tool-error matching on ≥ 10 real errors, at or above the 14/22 baseline; a digest observed after a real compaction; the injection noise measured before shipping (LRN-261); the measured oracle written only after the measurement (EVAL-056).
